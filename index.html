The Artist

About Me

Artist Photo

Nitin Verma

For over a decade, I have been captivated by the profound simplicity and expressive power of charcoal and graphite. These timeless mediums allow me to create depth, emotion, and soul in every stroke.

My work focuses on capturing the essence of famous personalities, the raw beauty of wildlife, and the serene tranquility of nature. Each piece is a meditation on light, shadow, and the stories they tell.

Based in India, my artworks are held in private collections across the country and internationally. I believe that true art connects the viewer to something deeper—a moment, a memory, an emotion.
